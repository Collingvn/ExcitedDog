# Excited Dog

A one-minute browser game built with three.js. You are an excited dog collecting golden bones while avoiding a sleeping cat.

## Play

Double-click `index.html`. It needs an internet connection because three.js loads from a CDN.

## How it works

- Collect golden bones by touching them. Each bone is 1 point.
- A cat sleeps on the field. Get too close and it wakes up and chases you, and the round is lost.
- One bone always sits just outside the cat's wake zone. Grab it and risk it, or skip it.
- Every 15 bones, the cat moves to a new spot.
- A round lasts 60 seconds. When time runs out, the game ends and shows your bones.

## Controls

Mouse or trackpad (the dog follows the pointer), arrow keys, or a tap on a touch screen.
